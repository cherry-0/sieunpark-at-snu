<p>Hi! I am a MS/PhD student in the Interdisciplinary Program in Artificial Intelligence at Seoul National University (SNU), advised by <a href="https://youngkilee.blogspot.com/p/about-me.html">Prof. Youngki Lee</a> in the <a href="https://hcs.snu.ac.kr/">Human-Centered Computer Systems Lab</a>.</p>

<p>I envision a future where intelligent AI assistants seamlessly support people throughout their daily lives on personal devices. To realize this vision, I aim to build AI systems that deeply understand users, act on their behalf, and remain trustworthy, transparent, and aligned with user expectations.</p>

<p>My research lies on two complementary directions:  
(1) <strong>Trustworthy AI Systems</strong> - designing privacy-preserving, transparent, and user-controllable AI systems that enable people to confidently adopt increasingly capable AI assistants, and  
(2) <strong>User Understanding and Agentic Intelligence</strong> - developing methods for long-term user modeling, intent understanding, and efficient agent execution, allowing AI systems to provide personalized assistance and reliably accomplish user goals</p>

<p>Currently, I am working on:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Inference-aware privacy</strong>: developing OS-level safeguards against hidden or unauthorized inferences by AI models inside apps.</li>
  <li><strong>LLM-powered mobile agents</strong>:     building and evaluating agentic systems that understand user intent and perform tasks across mobile applications. </li>
  <li><strong>Child learning support in home settings</strong>: designing scaffolding systems to support socially sensitive educational interactions, grounded in learning theory.</li>
  <li><strong>Personalized AI systems</strong>:     exploring how AI can build and leverage long-term understanding of users while respecting privacy and maintaining user trust.</li>
</ul>

<p>My goal is to create AI systems that are both capable and trustworthy—systems that understand users deeply, act effectively on their behalf, and give users meaningful control over how AI operates in their lives.</p>

<p>If you’d like to discuss research ideas or potential collaborations, feel free to reach out at: <strong>sieun.park [at] hcs [dot] snu [dot] ac [dot] kr</strong></p>

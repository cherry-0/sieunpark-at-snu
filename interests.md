## Research Projects

### Inference-Aware Privacy
- Developing OS-level safeguards to prevent hidden or unauthorized inferences by AI models
- Exploring user-facing mechanisms that make inference risks visible and manageable

### XR Affordance Augmentation
- Enabling 3D objects to gain on-demand functionality in XR through LLM- or VLM-guided interpretation
- Investigating how affordance generation can support collaboration and cross-spatial interaction

### LLM-Powered Mobile Agents
- Building and evaluating agentic pipelines for task automation and app interaction on mobile platforms
- Studying interaction design for reliable, human-aligned mobile agents

### Child Learning Support
- Designing incremental scaffolding systems for children’s storytelling in home settings
- Grounding system design in educational theory (e.g., ZPD) to balance guidance and autonomy
